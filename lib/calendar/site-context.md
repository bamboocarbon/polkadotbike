polkadotbike.com is a cycling tools and content site. Its assets are: gear
ratio calculators, Grand Tour climb data (Tour, Giro, Vuelta), and 3D
topographic climb visualisations with a plan/route/profile morph. It runs
affiliate programmes with Stay22, Booking.com and Travelpayouts. Audience
skews UK, road and gravel, 40+, data-interested. Instagram @polkadotbike.cc
plus a Facebook page. The site's known weakness is domain authority and
backlinks against incumbents like Sheldon Brown and Ritzelrechner.
