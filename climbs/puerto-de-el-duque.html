What gearing do you need?

Riding Puerto de El Duque at a steady 200W, a 75kg rider on an 8kg bike would climb at roughly 10.1 km/h — about 29 min to the top and a VAM of roughly 826 m/hr.

To turn 85 rpm at that speed you want something easier than a 34×36 — this gradient is beyond a standard road setup at that cadence. Anything harder and you will be grinding below 85 rpm for 4.8km.

Where it appears

Cat1 La Vuelta a España 2026 — Stage 20: La Calahorra → Collado del Alguacil
4.8km at 8.2% · summit 1,670m · 31.4km from the finish
Never before climbed in Vuelta history. Ascends the same Genil River valley as Alto de Hazallanas, with an identical final 2km finishing on the same road to the Sierra Nevada ski resort — but shorter and slightly easier, and without the 18-19% ramps that mark Hazallanas' opening kilometres after the technical descent from Güéjar Sierra.
